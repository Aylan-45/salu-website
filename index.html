For Salu

Dedicated to the girl who became one of the most important chapters of my life. For ten years of laughter, chaos, comfort, understanding and memories.

The Girl By The Railing

It was 2015.

A girl stood by a railing wearing a white dress.

I did not know her. She did not know me.

Yet somehow I found myself wondering:

"Will she ever be my friend?"

I never imagined that two years later, in 2017, we would end up in the same section and begin writing a story that would last a decade.

The Peace Between Pages

People ask for favorite memories.

The truth is that I do not have one.

Because every moment with Salu became a favorite.

What I remember most is a feeling.

The feeling of being completely myself. The feeling of peace. The feeling of forgetting the rest of the world for a while.

The Artist

One of my favorite things was always when Salu did my makeup.

Not because of the brushes. Not because of the colors.

But because those moments always felt like laughter, comfort and home.

The Language Of Silence

The strange thing about Salu is that she understands things I never say.

I do not know how she always knows what I need.

Before the explanation. Before the words. Before I even understand it myself.

She understands.

And that is a rare kind of magic.

A Decade

Ten years sounds small when written.

Just two words.

Yet inside those ten years live thousands of conversations, countless laughs, unforgettable memories and a friendship that became part of who I am.

Hidden Letter

Dear Salu,

This website was never meant to impress you.

It exists because memories fade. Screenshots disappear. Phones break. Time moves on.

But some people deserve a place where they are remembered.

Thank you for every conversation. Every laugh. Every memory. Every ordinary day that somehow became special because you were there.

For ten years you have been one of my favorite people.

Love,
Soumya
